# Housing section 5: household services and utilities

All pieces are decor in the editor's **Services** category (catalog 40217-40264, category 9, `housing_household_world.sql`,
generated by `tools/make_household.py`). Unlike other decor they are bought, not granted at house creation: trainers and the
auctioneer cost 100 g, stations and mailboxes 10 g, the rest 50 g.
Server: `HousingHousehold.inc` (the kinds extend `HousingCollections.inc`). Client: `Addons/EsteriaHousing/Household.lua` (storage chest window only).

| Spec | Pieces | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 Crafting | Anvil, Runic Anvil, Forge, Crystal Forge, Cooking Fire, Cooking Grill, Alchemy Lab, Black Anvil, Black Forge, Runeforge | A hidden spell focus (10 yd) sits beside each one, so recipes that need that station work. Skill, recipe and reagent checks are the game's own. Owner picks who may use it; others get "requires" (OnSpellCheckCast). |
| 5.2 Innkeeper | Innkeeper | Gossip "Make this house your home" with a confirmation that names the binding it replaces. Gives a **House Hearthstone** (item 9500200, spell 9500102, separate cooldown `EsteriaHousing.HearthCooldownMinutes`). Owner toggles "guests may bind". On use: in front of the innkeeper wherever it now stands (moved house or innkeeper). If the innkeeper is gone, the owner lands at the door. If access was revoked or the house is gone, the character goes to its ordinary hearth. Travel blocks are the same as Teleport: Home's. |
| 5.3 Bank | Banker (NPC), Bank Vault, Bank Chest | Opens the visitor's **own** bank (the core's bank, slots and rules). A vault or chest has a hidden banker beside it. |
| 5.4 Guild bank | Guild Vault | The core's guild vault object: guild ranks, withdrawal limits and logs are untouched. Entering the house grants nothing. |
| 5.5 Mail | Thunder Totem Mailbox, Explorer's Mailbox | The core's mail through a hidden mailbox: everyone sends and collects their own mail. |
| 5.6 Trainers | 14 profession trainers (40251-40264) | Bought from the catalog. Each teaches the union of every trainer of that profession (ranks and recipes) with the normal level, skill, prerequisite and cost checks. Named "<Profession> Trainer", renameable, with a customizable look. |
| 5.7 Repairs | Repair Smith (NPC), Repair Bench | The gossip line shows the final cost, the normal cost and the discount. The confirmation shows the money. Discount `EsteriaHousing.RepairDiscount` (default 10%, capped at 25%). Payment and durability are server-side (Player::DurabilityRepairAll). |
| 5.8 Auctioneer | Auctioneer | The core's auction house (deposits, fees, mail delivery unchanged). The owner sets the name, title, look and a greeting it says to visitors. It works while the owner is offline. |
| 5.9 Storage | Small (16), Storage Chest (28), Large (40 slots) | The window has Contents, Put In, Log and Settings tabs. Name, who may take out and who may put in (only me, anyone who can come in, friends, guild, group), and a log of deposits and withdrawals. Soulbound items go back only to the character who stored them, account-bound only to that account. Conjured, timed, refundable and still-tradeable loot are refused. **Contents travel**: pick a chest up and its contents are packed away; they come back when a chest of the same kind is placed. One server lock serializes every move, so two people cannot take the same item. |

Every NPC (innkeeper, banker, smith, auctioneer, trainers) is a figure like the mannequin. It has a pooled name, and the owner's gossip offers rename, retitle and the appearance customizer. The owner's gossip on every service sets **who may use this**. The server enforces it on the core's own packets too (mail, auction, bank, guild bank and trainer opcodes whose first field is the service's guid).

Known gaps:
- 5.2's retail cast visual is not ported. Retail Teleport Home is spell 1233637 (SpellVisual 86960). It plays kit 360, the classic Hearthstone cast, which ours keeps. It also plays kit 117122: two model effects (SpellVisualEffectName 33358, 32597) and a sound. The 12.1.5.70077 SpellVisualEffectName.db2 (FDID 897948) we fetched has no decodable model column, so those two models are unresolved. Fix: decode that table with a current WoWDBDefs layout for 12.1.5, take the two ModelFileDataIDs, and add SpellVisualKit and SpellVisualEffectName rows pointing at `retail\<fdid>.m2` in make_household.py (`HEARTH_VISUAL`).
- There are no co-owner or "trusted guest" roles in housing yet. Access uses the house's own groups (friends, guild, group, anyone who can enter).
- No property deletion or sale exists yet. When one is added, it must mail any packed chest contents to the owner. Until then nothing is ever lost: chests only pack away.
- Auctioneers use the neutral auction house when `AllowTwoSide.Interaction.Auction` is off (faction 35). The realm runs it on (one shared AH).
